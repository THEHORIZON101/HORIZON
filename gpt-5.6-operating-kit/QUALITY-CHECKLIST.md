# Quality checklist

Before accepting important GPT-5.6 work, check:

## Outcome

- The requested artifact or answer exists.
- It solves the real use case, not only the literal wording.
- “Done” is supported by observable conditions.

## Evidence

- Current or high-stakes claims were verified.
- Citations support the exact claims beside them.
- Inference, uncertainty, and source conflict are labeled.

## Coding and tools

- Changed behavior was tested.
- Builds, type-checks, or smoke tests ran where applicable.
- Interactive work was actually exercised.
- Tool failures and retries were not hidden.

## Files and visuals

- Documents, slides, PDFs, websites, and games were rendered or launched.
- Layout, clipping, spacing, responsiveness, and missing assets were inspected.
- Tables and charts use correct source data and units.

## Honesty

- The result does not inflate a sample into a universal score.
- Unverified behavior is labeled unverified.
- Remaining limitations are genuine and specific.
